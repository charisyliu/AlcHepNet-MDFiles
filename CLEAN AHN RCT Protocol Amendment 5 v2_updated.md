# Study Overview

## Scientific Objective

Evaluate whether **Anakinra plus zinc** improves outcomes compared with **prednisone** for adults hospitalized with severe alcoholic hepatitis while characterizing safety, biomarkers, and future endpoint development.

---

## Study Design

| Characteristic | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| Phase | Phase 2B |
| Study Type | Interventional |
| Design | Prospective |
| Allocation | Randomized |
| Blinding | Double-blind |
| Control | Active comparator with matching placebos |
| Centers | Eight U.S. academic medical centers |
| Enrollment | Approximately 258 participants |
| Randomization | 1:1 |
| Follow-up | 180 days |

# Study Population

## Target Population

Hospitalized adults with severe alcoholic hepatitis.

Major eligibility requirements include:

- Age ≥21 years
- Protocol-defined severe alcoholic hepatitis
- MELD 20–35
- Hospitalization
- Written informed consent
- Satisfaction of all eligibility criteria before randomization

---

## Exclusion Logic

Participants are excluded when another condition substantially alters:

- diagnosis,
- participant safety,
- treatment response,
- protocol compliance,
- or interpretation of study outcomes.

Major exclusion categories include:

- Alternative liver disease
- Severe infection
- Multi-organ failure
- Severe renal dysfunction
- Pregnancy
- Active malignancy meeting protocol criteria
- HIV infection
- Recent prohibited medications
- Medication allergy
- Other protocol-defined medical conditions

For atypical presentations, additional diagnostic evaluation (including liver biopsy when appropriate) may be performed before enrollment.

# Intervention

## Treatment Arms

### Arm 1

Standard care

Prednisone

Placebo Anakinra

Placebo zinc

---

### Arm 2

Standard care

Anakinra

Zinc sulfate

Placebo prednisone

---

## Treatment Administration

Participants receive protocol-assigned therapy in addition to standard clinical care.

Matching placebos maintain double blinding.

Participants may continue treatment after discharge following protocol training.

---

## Standard Clinical Care

Routine management may include treatment of:

- infection
- portal hypertension
- acute kidney injury
- gastrointestinal bleeding
- hepatic encephalopathy
- nutritional deficiencies
- alcohol use disorder

Management remains under the treating clinical team.

---

## Treatment Modification

The protocol uses fixed treatment regimens.

Temporary interruption or permanent discontinuation may occur when participant safety requires.

Participants are encouraged to continue study follow-up whenever feasible after stopping study medication.

---

## Day 7 Lille Rule

Day 7 Lille score determines continuation of prednisone (or matching placebo).

If Lille score exceeds the protocol threshold, prednisone (or placebo) is discontinued while zinc (or placebo) continues to preserve blinding.

# Study Timeline & Data Collection

## Study Timeline

Participant activities follow four sequential phases.

| Phase | Primary Activities |
|------|---------------------|
| Screening | Consent, eligibility confirmation, baseline assessments |
| Day 0 (Randomization) | Randomization, baseline clinical evaluation, biospecimen collection, treatment initiation |
| Treatment | Scheduled efficacy and safety assessments while receiving study medication |
| Follow-up | Clinical outcomes, laboratory monitoring, adverse events, survival, and biospecimen collection through Day 180 |

Day 0 (randomization) serves as the reference time point for all efficacy, safety, and time-to-event analyses.

---

## Scheduled Visits

| Visit | Primary Purpose |
|--------|-----------------|
| Screening | Eligibility and baseline characterization |
| Day 0 | Randomization and treatment initiation |
| Day 3 | Early safety assessment |
| Day 7 | Lille score assessment and early treatment response |
| Day 14 | Completion of Anakinra/placebo injections |
| Day 28 | Clinical response assessment |
| Day 60 | Continued efficacy and safety follow-up |
| Day 90 | Primary endpoint assessment |
| Day 180 | Final follow-up |

Visit windows follow protocol-defined allowances.

Participants who discontinue study medication are encouraged to continue protocol follow-up whenever feasible.

# Study Outcomes

## Outcome Structure

The protocol evaluates efficacy, safety, organ dysfunction, infection, renal outcomes, biomarkers, and development of improved clinical endpoints.

The primary endpoint is **90-day survival**. Secondary and exploratory outcomes provide additional information on disease progression, treatment response, organ failure, and biological mechanisms.

---

## Primary Endpoint

**90-day all-cause survival**

This is the primary efficacy endpoint.

The primary comparison is between:

- Anakinra + zinc
- Prednisone

---

## Secondary Outcomes

Major secondary outcomes include:

- 30-day survival
- 180-day survival
- Change in MELD score
- Change in Lille score
- Acute kidney injury
- Multi-organ failure
- SOFA and modified SOFA
- Infection
- Sepsis
- Septic shock
- ICU transfer
- Mechanical ventilation
- Renal replacement therapy
- Hemodynamic support
- Changes in liver function

These outcomes characterize clinical response, organ dysfunction, and disease progression.

---

## Infection and Sepsis Outcomes

The protocol specifically evaluates:

- Type of infection
- Sepsis
- Septic shock
- qSOFA-related measures

Infection is an important clinical outcome because severe alcoholic hepatitis itself carries substantial infectious risk and study treatment may also influence infection-related events.

---

## Renal and Organ Dysfunction Outcomes

Renal and organ dysfunction outcomes include:

- Development of acute kidney injury
- Progression of renal dysfunction
- SOFA score
- Modified SOFA score
- Multi-organ failure
- Need for renal replacement therapy
- Need for mechanical ventilation
- Need for hemodynamic support

These outcomes reflect deterioration or recovery beyond survival alone.

---

## Exploratory Biomarker Outcomes

Exploratory analyses include measures related to:

- Gut permeability
- Endotoxin exposure
- Pro-inflammatory cytokines
- Chemokines
- Immune and inflammatory activity

These analyses are intended to improve understanding of severe alcoholic hepatitis biology and treatment response.

---

## Composite Endpoint Development

The protocol seeks to develop and validate improved clinical endpoints for future alcoholic hepatitis trials.

The proposed composite concept includes:

- Death
- Worsening SOFA score
- Worsening MELD score

The protocol describes worsening as increases of at least 2 points for SOFA and MELD in the proposed composite endpoint.

This endpoint is exploratory and intended for future endpoint development rather than serving as the primary efficacy endpoint.

---

# Safety & Statistical Analysis

## Safety Monitoring

Participant safety is monitored throughout study participation using scheduled clinical assessments, laboratory monitoring, adverse event surveillance, and independent oversight.

Safety follow-up continues through protocol-defined follow-up whenever feasible, including after study medication discontinuation.

Major safety assessments include:

- Physical examination
- Vital signs
- Clinical status
- Laboratory monitoring
- Medication review
- Treatment compliance
- Adverse event surveillance
- Infection monitoring
- Organ dysfunction assessment

An independent **Data and Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB)** periodically reviews accumulated safety data and may recommend study continuation, modification, or termination.

---

## Adverse Events

### Adverse Event (AE)

An adverse event is any unfavorable medical occurrence after enrollment regardless of its relationship to study treatment.

For each AE, investigators document:

- Description
- Onset and resolution
- Severity
- Seriousness
- Relationship to study treatment
- Action taken
- Outcome

### Serious Adverse Event (SAE)

SAEs include protocol-defined events such as:

- Death
- Life-threatening illness
- Hospitalization or prolonged hospitalization
- Persistent disability
- Congenital anomaly
- Other medically important events

Severity and seriousness are distinct concepts and should not be interpreted as equivalent.

---

## Major Safety Risks

The protocol places particular emphasis on monitoring:

- Infection
- Sepsis
- Septic shock
- Acute kidney injury
- Multi-organ failure
- Injection-site reactions
- Hypersensitivity reactions
- Liver disease progression
- Death

These events may reflect either progression of severe alcoholic hepatitis or effects of study treatment.

# Statistical Analysis

## Analysis Populations

Three principal analysis populations are defined.

| Population | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Intention-to-Treat (ITT) | Primary efficacy analysis; all randomized participants analyzed according to assigned treatment |
| Per-Protocol (PP) | Supportive efficacy analyses among participants without major protocol deviations |
| Safety Population | All participants receiving at least one dose of study medication |

---

## Primary Analysis

The primary efficacy analysis evaluates **90-day all-cause survival** using the ITT population.

The protocol specifies survival analysis methods including:

- Kaplan–Meier estimation
- Log-rank testing
- Cox proportional hazards regression

---

## Secondary Analyses

Secondary analyses evaluate protocol-defined efficacy outcomes using methods appropriate for the outcome type, including:

- Time-to-event analyses
- Binary outcome analyses
- Continuous outcome analyses
- Longitudinal analyses

Exploratory biomarker analyses are hypothesis-generating and are not considered confirmatory efficacy analyses.

---

## Missing Data

The protocol emphasizes minimizing missing data through:

- Scheduled follow-up
- Telephone follow-up when appropriate
- Continued collection of outcome data after treatment discontinuation whenever feasible

The protocol does not specify additional missing-data procedures beyond those explicitly described.

---

## Interpretation Principles

When using this knowledge base:

- Base efficacy conclusions on the ITT analysis unless the protocol specifies otherwise.
- Distinguish efficacy analyses from safety analyses.
- Distinguish confirmatory analyses from exploratory analyses.
- Do not infer statistical methods that are not explicitly described in the protocol.
- If a requested analytical method is absent from the protocol, state:

> **The protocol does not specify this statistical method.**
